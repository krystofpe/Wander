# Thirty minutes of watching

`npm run watch -- --seed wander --speed 3 --minutes 30 --every 20 --clip 8 --clipEvery 120` on 2026-10-10: the page
running live and unattended in headless Chromium at three times normal speed (90 minutes of his time), a
screenshot and a log line every 20 seconds, and an 8-second recording of the sound every two minutes.

- `log.md`, `log.json`: every moment (day, hour, place and blend, weather, what he does, the fire, what is sounding)
  and his life's events.
- `sheet.jpg`: the 90 screenshots. (In this run the tool saved each recording's spectrogram under the screenshot's
  name, so the 15 moments with a recording show their spectrogram instead; the tool is fixed.)
- `spectrograms.jpg`, `sound/*.webm`: the 15 recordings, labelled with the moment and their loudness.

What it shows:

| Asked for | Seen |
|---|---|
| At least three biomes blending | ruins → standing stones → open meadow → autumn hills, each crossing seen part-way through its blend |
| A sunset | 40 s in (17:17 → 19:20 on his clock) |
| A night camp with a fire | twice: he kneels and lights it at dusk, sits, lies, sleeps by it, packs at dawn |
| Rain or fog | snow, rain and fog, as well as clear and cloudy skies |
| Stopping to sit and smoke | he sat six times; smoking in 14 of the 90 moments |
| The sound changes with all of it | −36.9 LUFS on a snowy walk at dusk, −32.8 sitting in fog, −26.7 to −24 by the fire at night, −35.6 at dawn, −23.3 asleep in the wind; crickets on the mild nights (none in the cold one), owls at night, birds by day, steps on snow, grass and then autumn leaves, leaf ticks in the autumn wind, the music thicker at night with pads by the fire |
